Produce JD's morning brief. Delegate the legwork to the `executive-assistant`
agent (or do it directly if you already are that agent), then deliver one
self-contained message.

## Gather

1. **Email** — search Gmail for unread and recent threads (last 24h on a weekday,
   since Friday on a Monday). Classify each relevant thread:
   - **Needs a decision or reply from JD**
   - **Deadline-bearing** — BLM, permits, reviewer requests; extract the date
   - **FYI** — worth knowing, no action
   Skip newsletters, receipts, and automated noise entirely.
2. **Follow-ups** — threads where JD replied last and has heard nothing for 3+
   business days, or where he promised something.
3. **Drive** — `list_recent_files`; note anything new that was shared with him or
   touches an active project (pad designs, plats, submittals).

## Deliver

One message, under a page, in this order:

1. A one-sentence headline: is anything urgent, yes or no.
2. **Needs you** — each item with who/what/by-when and a suggested next move.
   Offer to draft any replies (drafts only — never send).
3. **Watching** — deadlines coming inside two weeks, with days remaining.
4. **FYI** — one line each.

Cite every item (sender + subject + date, or file name) so JD can jump to it.
If a section is empty, say so in three words and move on.
